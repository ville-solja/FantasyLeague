Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-06

This policy describes what information Kana Cards (the Kanaliiga Fantasy League app, "the Service") collects, why, and how it's used — including through the Kanaliiga Kana-Cards Twitch Extension.

Information we collect

  • Account information — username, email address, and a hashed password when you register directly on the Service. We never store plaintext passwords.
  • Linked Dota account — if you link your own OpenDota/Steam account ID, so the app can associate your Fantasy roster with real match statistics.
  • Twitch soft account — if you press Join Kana Cards in the Twitch Extension, we create a game account (a "soft account") that holds only the opaque, per-extension Twitch user id Twitch's platform provides, your game progress (cards, tokens, roster, points) and timestamps (when you joined and when you last played). It has no username, email or password.
  • Optional real Twitch user id — when you join, Twitch asks whether to share your identity with the Extension. If you agree, we also store your real Twitch user id (a number, not your Twitch username or email), so a later Kana Cards website account can be connected to the same progress. If you decline, the soft account uses the opaque id only.
  • Twitch connection — when you press Connect Twitch on your Profile, you sign in on Twitch's own page and Twitch confirms your Twitch user id to us. We store only that Twitch user id (a number, not your Twitch username or email) on your website account, and, once you use the Twitch panel, the opaque per-extension id, so the panel and token drops use your website account. The sign-in tokens Twitch returns are not stored: they are discarded as soon as your Twitch user id is confirmed. Disconnecting on your Profile removes both ids from your account. Website accounts linked earlier with a one-time code keep the opaque id until they disconnect.
  • Gameplay data — your card collection, token balance, roster picks, and fantasy scores, all generated by using the Service.
  • Match and player statistics — sourced from the public OpenDota API for matches in leagues the Service tracks. This data describes public esports matches, not individual Service users.
  • Basic technical data — standard web server logs (IP address, timestamps, request paths) generated by normal operation, used only for debugging and abuse prevention.

Twitch Extension data specifically

The Kanaliiga Kana-Cards Twitch Extension is a game you play inside the Twitch panel with your Twitch login. Anyone can see the live results without an account. Pressing Join Kana Cards creates the soft account described above; we store your opaque Twitch id, your game progress and timestamps to run the game: to keep your collection and roster, score your roster each week, and include you in token drops while you watch. Twitch is never asked for your Twitch username, email, or follower/subscription data.

Kana Cards never asks for your Twitch or website password inside Twitch. The Extension has no password field. Connecting Twitch happens on the website, where you sign in on Twitch's own page; Kana Cards never sees your Twitch password.

If you played in the panel before connecting Twitch on the website, your Profile offers to add that Twitch collection (cards and tokens) to your website account once. We keep a record of each such merge for 30 days so a mistaken merge can be undone, then delete it.

Soft accounts never appear on leaderboards, season standings or any public page, and Twitch ids are never shown to other users.

The Extension may post a message to the broadcaster's Twitch chat when a match MVP is selected (e.g. "Match MVP: PlayerName! 3 viewers received a token."), using Twitch's Extension Chat capability. It names the MVP and how many viewers received a token, never viewer names. No other chat activity is read, stored, or posted by the Extension.

Leaving the Twitch game

Open the panel's Settings and press Leave Kana Cards. For a soft account this deletes the account and all its game data (cards, points, roster, tokens) immediately. For a website account, Leave only removes the panel link and keeps the website account and its Twitch connection; disconnect Twitch on your website Profile to remove the connection. Soft accounts with no activity for 365 days are deleted automatically.

How we use this information

  • To operate the Service: authenticate you, track your cards/tokens/roster, compute fantasy scores, and run the weekly leaderboard.
  • To operate the Twitch Extension: keep your soft account's game progress, track token-drop eligibility while you watch, and show your tokens, cards and roster in the panel.
  • To send account-related email (e.g. password reset) via our SMTP provider — these emails contain only what's necessary for that purpose.
  • We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for advertising or marketing.

Third-party services

  • OpenDota API — public Dota 2 match/player statistics; no Service account data is sent to OpenDota.
  • Twitch Extensions platform — hosts the Extension's frontend and issues the identity/authorization tokens the Extension backend validates.
  • SMTP relay — used solely to deliver password-reset email.

Data retention and deletion

Account data is retained for as long as your account exists. Twitch soft accounts are kept while you play and deleted after 365 days without activity, or at once when you leave in the panel's Settings. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us using the details below.

Cookies and sessions

The Service uses a session cookie to keep you logged in. It is not used for cross-site tracking or advertising.

Children's privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Service changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to the Service operator via the contact details on kana-cards.com.
