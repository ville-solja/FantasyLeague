Kana Cards — Setup

This extension adds the Kana Cards fantasy game to your stream.

For viewers

The panel below your stream shows live fantasy results to every viewer. Viewers logged in to Twitch can join with one button, then draw cards, set a weekly roster and win tokens from MVP drops, all inside the panel.

For broadcasters — MVP selection

MVP selection and token drops are in Stream Manager → Quick Actions.

  1. Open Stream Manager (dashboard.twitch.tv → Stream Manager)
  2. Find the Kana Cards tile in the Quick Actions bar at the bottom
  3. Click it to open MVP selection — available during and after matches

Confirming an MVP posts one chat announcement with the MVP's name and how many viewers received a token. No viewer names are posted. The extension posts nothing else and never reads chat.

Quick Actions are only visible while you are live or in Stream Manager. The MVP button will not appear on the channel page.

EBS URL
