# Plan: Card Visuals Rollback

## Context
#218 replaced the card art with generated "layout A" templates: a rarity-coloured frame with two side pillars that hold the HTML accolade pips (right) and tag pips (left), plus a generated Holo template (commit `da89798`). Issue #228 asks to return the cards to the visuals in use when accolades were introduced (#50, #53, #217): the original illustrated templates from commit `a8eeb4d` and the card renderer as it was just before #218 (`da89798^`). The accolades then leave the card face and move to the card popup (the card viewer), where each one is shown with its details, such as the season, division and team of a season win. The tag pips (#217) leave the card face too, because they clutter the art as well (product-owner review); they move to the same popup. The card art stays clean, and the symbols stay one tap away.

**What #218 changed, and what goes back:**

| Area | #218 (now) | After this plan |
|---|---|---|
| Templates | `assets/Card_Template_{Common,Rare,Epic,Legendary}.png` generated by `backend/scripts/make_card_templates.py`; `Card_Template_Holo.png` added | The original four templates restored from `da89798^`; `Card_Template_Holo.png` and the generator removed |
| Renderer | `backend/image.py` drawing layout A: 368 × 368 portrait slot, 100 × 60 logo slot, rarity edge colour, legendary and Holo shine | `backend/image.py` restored from `da89798^`: 350 px portrait circle, 104 px logo circle, and the #53 Holo path (`_load_template` falls back to the Legendary template with the generated iridescent sheen when no Holo template exists) |
| Accolade pips on cards | `.card-pips` over the right pillar on every card face | **Removed from card faces.** Shown in the card viewer instead (below) |
| Tag pips on cards (#217) | `.card-tag-pips` over the left pillar, sized in container units | **Removed from card faces** (product-owner review: they clutter the art). Shown in the card viewer instead (below) |
| Rank medal (#221) | Website badge only; not on the PNG | Unchanged |

**Where the pips stay:** only card faces lose them. The player popup header keeps its tag and accolade pips, and the Players list Titles column, the Teams tab, the team popup roster and the Hall of fame keep their accolade pips and chips; they aren't card art.

**Decisions** (change them in review if needed):
- **Card viewer accolades section:** the card viewer (`#revealModal`) gets an **Accolades** section under the card, replacing today's chip row (`#revealAccolades`). One line per accolade, newest season first, champions first within a season:
  - **Division champion:** "S15 Div 1 Champion · with <team name>".
  - **Point-based accolades** (Most Points, Best Average, Best Single Match, and Fantasy Winner when it carries a value): "S15 Most Points · 812.3 pts".
  - **Played:** one summary line, "Played 7 seasons · S4–S16" (counted from Season 4, as in #212), with the per-season list in a disclosure.
  - Each line keeps its symbol and plate colour from `ACCOLADE_KINDS`, so it matches the pips used elsewhere. No accolades: the section is left out.
- **Card viewer tags section:** above the accolades, a **Tags** section lists the linked user's tags in `TAG_SYMBOLS` order, each with its symbol and text: "Kana Cards admin", "Caster", then other tags by their label. It uses the same `/accolades` `tags` data as #217 (Steam-verified links only, no user identity), and `season_winner` stays out. No tags: the section is left out.
- **Draw reveal:** during the draw animation both sections stay hidden, as the chips are today. It appears once the flip has finished, so a drawn champion's titles show at the end of the reveal.
- **Two helpers:** new `accoladeDetailsHtml(accolades)` and `tagDetailsHtml(tags)` in `frontend/app-globals.js` build the sections from the same `/accolades` data. No new endpoint: `team_name`, `value`, `season_label` and the played rows are already in `GET /accolades`.
- **Tests from #218:** `test_issue_218_card_formatting.py` pins layout A. Its tests are replaced by tests of the restored renderer. The #107 and #50 tests that #218 edited go back to their `da89798^` assertions, adjusted for anything added since.
- **The #218 plan and docs stay as history:** the card-formatting doc says layout A was rolled back by #228 and links this plan. The layout A design canvas stays available if a new card design is tried later.
- **No server cache to clear:** card images are sent with `Cache-Control: no-store`, so the old art shows on the next load. The Twitch panel draws its own card art (it loads no card PNG), so it is unaffected.

Resolves GitHub issue #228.

## User Stories

### Cards Return to the Original Art
**User story**
As a player, I want my cards to look like the original illustrated cards again, so that the card art isn't crowded by symbols.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Templates:** `GET /cards/{id}/image` renders on the original templates (`Card_Template_{Common,Rare,Epic,Legendary}.png` as of `da89798^`), byte-identical to those files.
- **Renderer:** the portrait circle, logo circle, name, team and modifier placement match the renderer before #218, including the blank team-logo placeholder (#107).
- **Holo:** a Holo legendary uses the Legendary template with the generated iridescent sheen (#53), and `Card_Template_Holo.png` no longer exists.
- **No generator:** `backend/scripts/make_card_templates.py` is removed.
- **Card size:** the image stays 597 × 845, so the roster, the reveal and the #205 draw animation keep their sizes.
- **Everywhere:** My Team, the collection, the draw reveal, the card viewer and the Weekly Report (which opens cards in the card viewer) all show the restored art. The Twitch panel draws its own card art and loads no card PNG, so it is unaffected.

### Accolades and Tags Leave the Card Face
**User story**
As a player, I want accolade and tag symbols off the card art, so that the cards look clean.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **No accolade pips on cards:** no `.card-pips` overlay on any card face: My Team roster and bench, the collection, the draw reveal and the card viewer.
- **No tag pips on cards:** no `.card-tag-pips` overlay on any card face either.
- **Elsewhere unchanged:** the player popup header keeps its tag and accolade pips; the Players list, the Teams tab, the team popup and the Hall of fame keep their accolade pips and chips.
- **No dead code:** `cardPipsHtml` and the card-face pip CSS (`.card-pips`, `.card-tag-pips`) are removed if nothing else uses them; `accoladePipsHtml` and `tagPipsHtml` stay for the views above.

### Accolade and Tag Details in the Card Popup
**User story**
As a player, I want to see a card's tags and accolades with their details when I open it, so that I can see who the player is and which seasons they won, and with which team.

**Acceptance criteria**
- **Tags:** opening a card in the card viewer shows a **Tags** section under the card when the linked user has tags: admin first ("Kana Cards admin"), then "Caster", then other tags by label, each with its symbol; never `season_winner`. Only Steam-verified links count, and no user identity is shown (#217 rules).
- **Accolades section:** below the tags, an **Accolades** section, one line per accolade, newest season first, champions first within a season.
- **Details:**
  - champion lines name the season, division and team ("S15 Div 1 Champion · with Team Name");
  - point-based accolades show their value ("S15 Most Points · 812.3 pts");
  - Played is one line with the count and season range, with the per-season list in a disclosure.
- **Look:** each line uses its kind's symbol and plate colour from `ACCOLADE_KINDS`; the symbol is decorative and the line's text carries the meaning.
- **Draw reveal:** both sections are hidden during the draw animation and shown once the flip has finished.
- **Empty:** a player with no tags or no accolades shows no section for it and no empty space.
- **Phone width:** the section wraps with no sideways scroll.

### Step 1 — Restore the card art
```bash
git checkout da89798^ -- assets/Card_Template_Common.png assets/Card_Template_Rare.png \
  assets/Card_Template_Epic.png assets/Card_Template_Legendary.png backend/image.py
git rm assets/Card_Template_Holo.png backend/scripts/make_card_templates.py
```
Check that nothing merged after #218 depends on the layout A renderer (slot constants, the shine helpers).

### Step 2 — Pips off the card faces
Remove the accolade and tag pip overlays from `_cardSlotHTML`, `_renderCardAccolades` and any collection or Weekly Report card markup, and their card-face CSS.

### Step 3 — Tags and Accolades sections in the card viewer
Add `tagDetailsHtml` and `accoladeDetailsHtml`, the section markup and styles; show them after the flip on a draw.

### Step 4 — Tests and docs

- **Test copy:** a common, rare, epic and legendary card each show the original art; a Holo legendary shows the original legendary art with the sheen.
- **My Team:** cards show no accolade or tag pips.
- **Card viewer tags:** opening a caster's card shows "Caster" with the mic symbol in the Tags section.
- **Card viewer:** opening last season's champion shows "S15 Div 1 Champion · with <team>" in the Accolades section; a player with no accolades shows no section.
- **Draw:** drawing a champion's card shows the section only after the flip.
- **Twitch panel:** unaffected; it draws its own card art and loads no card PNG.
