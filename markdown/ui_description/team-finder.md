# Team Finder tab (#220)

Tab button after Schedule (and Finals), before How to Play, hidden unless `GET /config` reports `team_signup_open` (a signup period is open and its closing time hasn't passed). Admins see the tab at any time. Opening the tab loads `GET /team-signup`.

**Style:** the Kana Cards design system (`colors_and_type.css` tokens, "States and emphasis" in `.claude/skills/README.md`); layout A of the design canvas `design/ui_kits/fantasy_web/team-finder.html`. Cards are same-size `.panel` surfaces; badges are the shared `.status-badge`; medals the shared `.rank-medal` (without the star marks, the name carries them); the viewer's own listing is `.row-self`; filter and role chips use the shared selected state (accent border, inverted on hover or focus).

- **Header:** "<period label> signup" eyebrow, "Find a team", "Closes <date>" when the period has a closing time, and a button: **Log in to list your team** (logged out, opens the login dialog), **List your team** or **Edit your listing** (scrolls to the form).
- **Closed:** "Signup is closed." (admins: "Open a period in Admin › Settings › Season › Team signup."); no filters, cards or form.
- **Filters** (in the browser, nothing is ranked): Players needed (Any, 1 or more … 4), Open role chips Pos 1–5 (any match), a medal range from Herald to Immortal on the team's average medal (teams with no medals always pass), and "N partial teams".
- **Card grid:** `repeat(auto-fit, minmax(320px, 1fr))`, one column at phone width, in the server's daily shuffle. Each card: team name ("Your team" badge on the own one), "N listed · updated <date>", players needed as the one large number ("needed"; muted at 0), open-role tags ("No open roles listed"), one player row per player and an "Open slot" row up to five, the note, and a footer with **Contact**: the contact for logged-in users, "Log in to see contact details" otherwise.
- **Player row:** name (or the id and an **Unknown player** badge), "(You)" for the viewer's Steam-verified player, the medal ("Unranked" when none), and badges: **Verified** (the captain's own Steam-verified player) or **Listed by captain**, **Also listed by another team** (`--k-warn`), and **Not on this team** on the viewer's own row in another captain's listing (asks for confirmation). At 760 px and below the badges move to their own line under the name and medal.
- **Full teams (N):** a collapsed `<details>` after the grid, compact cards with the player rows and contact.
- **Captain form** (logged in, period open): Team name (counter /32, "No links"); Players: a Dota id or Steam64 id field with **Add** (Enter adds; several ids separated by spaces or commas), errors "Dota ids are digits only", "Already on your team", "Seven players at most"; rows with a spinner while looking up (batched, debounced `GET /team-signup/lookup`), then name, id, "from Steam64", medal or **Unknown player**, Verified / Listed by captain and **Remove** (the own verified player shows "You" and can't be removed); "N/7 players" and the claims note. Open roles chips Pos 1–5, Note (/280, no links), Contact (required), **Team full** toggle with its help line, **Save listing** and **Delete listing** (own listing only, confirmation). A `422` shows the server's reason; `429` "Too many saves. Wait a minute and save again."; `409` (the period closed meanwhile) shows "Signup closed" and makes the form read-only.
- Every user-written string (team names, player names, notes, contacts) is escaped.

Admin side: Admin › Settings › Season › **Team signup** (see `admin.md`). Details: `markdown/features/core/team-signup.md`.
